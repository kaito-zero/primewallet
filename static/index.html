Forgot your passphrase?

      

        There's no recovery for this. primechain wallets don't have a
        seed phrase -- the passphrase directly encrypts the signing key,
        and there's no separate backup secret that unlocks it another
        way. If it's really gone, the wallet(s) at the current workdir
        can't be opened again.
      

      

        What you can do: point this app at a different (or new) workdir
        and set up a fresh wallet there. Nothing at the current workdir
        gets touched or deleted -- it just sits on disk in case the
        passphrase turns up later.
